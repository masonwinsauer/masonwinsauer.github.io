Memory is an exercise in apophenia, aphaneia, pareidolia.
Patterns in the patternless, the noise.
Patterns in the patternful, the structured.

Faces found in the wildflowers, the bread.

Memory holds the same value as a dream.
They are each formed not by the head but by the heart.
If the dream made me to know the truth?
~Tie into belief from truth or from dreams?
