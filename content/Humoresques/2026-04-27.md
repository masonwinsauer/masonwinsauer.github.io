How lonely it can be to be the only one to know the truth. Are we not each singularly despondent in the truth of ourselves as others fumble about trying to make sense of our discordant actions, contradictory words, broken promises. They survey with horror and disgust a contract they pretend we've signed, noting every change of behavior that does not match their canon of illusions. 

How funny they look, if we can see beyond the disappointment.
