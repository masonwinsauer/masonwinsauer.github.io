I've been seeing someone
he's in my peripheral 
he seems nice
he doesn't like to talk much
just lets the world consume itself in rhetorical fire 
he doesn't seem to mind that no one asks for his opinion but maybe I should
I think it would be measured, insightful, compassionate, careful not to offend
I think he would make me feel seen
I think he would stop short to let me speak when he saw my lips do that thing
I think he would laugh at the absurdity of the world we created
I think his laugh would make it a bit better
maybe he wants to laugh more but feels this world hasn't earned his levity
maybe he thinks that serious effort will start changing minds
maybe he laughs at himself for thinking such thoughts
maybe it's just a tree branch
