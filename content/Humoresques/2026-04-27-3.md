Under uniformly grey-yellow skies, I told her it couldn't be.

She spoke something in return, but the white noise of heavy drops drowned her out. Maybe I just wasn't listening. I watched her mouth form... something - It would be logical to assume words. 

Lightning bolts were grounding all around us - too close for thunder to be thunder. Our hair would have stood on end if it hadn't been bludgeoned by slick gravity. Our eardrums would have ruptured but for her voice, the antidote, destructive interference. Our sight would have burned away if our eyes had not been so firmly set upon each others face.

Death followed. No, not death, the other thing.
