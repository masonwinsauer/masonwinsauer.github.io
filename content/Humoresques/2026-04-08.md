I have forged a double edged sword of regret. I sought out only the most impure of ores. The brittle flaws, when exposed to clattering resistance, chip away. The cuts become more jagged. Where once there was surgical precision, experience now tears.

I whip the blade fore and aft, the beasts of self attacking from all angles. I exhaust myself, battling, crying with closed eyes. I awaken wounded, but still adrift.
