<em>Withdrawals</em>

My therapist says my needs aren't being met. I don't want to believe that.

**I** want to believe that I'm a selfish prick. Turns out she can't stop me.

Here's the cycle:

1. A desire for something normal
2. Guilt for wanting the normal something
3. Fear of what the wanting says about me
4. Withdrawal, shame, denial of self
5. Anger for repeating the cycle again
6. Repeat the cycle again
