I like that deer sleep near me.

I find little ovals of spiraling grass, gently tamped down as warm fur finds safety and comfort among papery birches.

Fret they not of days yet to be.

They truly rest.

No expectations.

They do not assign themselves worth based on deeds! Ha! How human of us.
