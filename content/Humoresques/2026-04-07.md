I spoke with D today. They were very understanding. They listened patiently as I recounted my petty fears - That the rest of my life will be a series of falsely presenting emotions, that true love only exists for the most ignorant, boring, or selfless, that we all just want each other to be happy, but will never understand how to cultivate the type of self love that surpasses the fleeting nature of happiness.

They laughed at the last one. They've seen enough people die of it, I guess.

The laugh was comforting, though. Not the characteristics of the sound - It would even border on chillingly dry. It almost sounded like a boulder rolling, bouncing towards a village it always secretly hated. 

That someone like her can still find humor in our affairs confirms a great unknown of the universe. It isn't uncaring. It isn't disinterested. It just has a morbid sense of humor.
