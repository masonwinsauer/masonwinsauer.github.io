They called me "peacemaker". <br> <br>
I don't think they meant it. <br>
They saw fear, longing for love, denial of self and encouraged it. Named it. <br>
A child so scared of anger that he would do anything within his power to make it stop. <br>
Smiling subservience to unrepentant scorn. <br>
Elasticity becomes plasticity.  <br>
Irreversible deformations. <br>
Identities blooming with saturation turn white at the point of stress. <br>
Careless repetition generates the heat that breaks us down.
