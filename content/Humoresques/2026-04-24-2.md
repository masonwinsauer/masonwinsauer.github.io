To the romance and perfection of the cigarette.

There's something about smoking a good old fashioned cigarette. Everyone knows the drawbacks - This ain't the '50s. It's duality is it's balance. Fleeting bliss, fleeting pain. There seems to be a conversation around whether the effects experienced later in life outweigh those of the now. That once the proverbial bullet fucks the barrel, you'll want to take it all back. Recency bias. 

Death becomes us, anyway. A dreamy palate of pallor <do better>.

Shan't we wear our best today?
