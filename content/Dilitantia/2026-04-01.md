This is a betrayal of everything I have been. The tome of heartbreak now leaking its re-liquefied ink into the drinking water.

Individual black drops form swirls of confusion and mistrust. Dense packets of once well formed thoughts take hundreds of ephemeral shapes in mere seconds - Laughing at those with but one form. They appear as one thing, then another ad infinitum until their laughter jostles them apart, dissipating into lost memories. Sulfates spread too thin. When the water moves quickly, it is nothing to anyone - Just another pollutant.

Hi. That was a dramatic start, eh?

The truth is, I don't like being known, but I hate not being understood. I push away those who get the closest because I fear they will see something in me that I have yet to reckon with myself. Preemptive character strikes bombard my esteem, chipping away at any semblance of worth.

When it comes to relationships, well, let's be honest here, Mason, relationship, I ache for what cannot be forced. There is no greater love than to be seen, heard, and if not understood, to feel that your partner wants to - needs to help you feel comfortable in your own skin. When someone takes the effort to understand, it validates the thing that you are.

It is natural to resent losing what you had. It is natural to misunderstand. It is natural to hear one thing when another is said. 

Maybe it is unnatural in the face of these realities to look past them, to push through the sheddings to find the raw, vulnerable thing that has emerged.

Maybe.

Maybe I just haven't been loved unconditionally yet.

Maybe I haven't loved unconditionally yet.

Maybe loving yourself unconditionally is the only way. 

To subsist on your own love, though? The therapists dream.

To not seek or expect a specific form of care from another?

What then is the point of the relationship? Security? Sex? Tax benefits? 

Every time I wonder what time it is, it's 10:00.
